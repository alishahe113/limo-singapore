Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026. Limo Singapore Pte. Ltd. is committed to protecting your personal data and upholding absolute travel confidentiality.

1. Commitment to Singapore PDPA

Limo Singapore Pte. Ltd. strictly adheres to the provisions of the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). We respect your privacy and ensure all personal data collected in the course of chauffeur dispatch, airport tracking, or corporate billing is handled with the highest level of security and confidentiality.

2. Information We Collect

To provide first-class limousine transfers, we may collect the following information when you place a reservation:

  • Identity Details: Full Name, Title, Corporate Designation.
  • Contact Details: Phone / WhatsApp Number, Email Address.
  • Travel Details: Flight Numbers, Changi Terminal, Pickup & Dropoff Addresses, Special Preferences (child seats, beverages).
  • Payment Records: Credit card billing details processed via PCI-DSS encrypted payment gateways.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized for operational execution:

  • Dispatching assigned chauffeurs and sending automated SMS / WhatsApp driver arrival notifications.
  • Monitoring real-time flight arrival schedules at Changi Airport.
  • Generating official GST tax invoices and corporate monthly account statements.
  • Customer support inquiries and lost-and-found recovery dispatch.

4. Zero Data Monetization & VVIP Confidentiality

We NEVER sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal data or travel itineraries to third-party marketing companies. All chauffeurs operate under binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) to safeguard the identity and privacy of state dignitaries, celebrities, and corporate executives.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We employ industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption, secure firewall architecture, and strict physical access controls. Billing details are processed via Tier-1 PCI-DSS compliant financial gateways.

6. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

If you have any questions regarding your personal data, wish to request data correction, or request data erasure, please contact our Data Protection Officer:
